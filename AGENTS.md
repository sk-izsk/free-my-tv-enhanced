# Free My TV Enhanced: guide for AI assistants

The full guide is [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md): read it first and follow it (it is written for any assistant, not only
Claude Code). Its first section says what this fork adds and what to tell people after installing it. Most people ask
in plain words, like "Install Free My TV Enhanced (github.com/sk-izsk/free-my-tv-enhanced) on my TV at 192.168.1.50.
I live in Lisbon.", and don't know what a terminal is: explain each step simply, and ask before anything that changes
their TV or computer.
